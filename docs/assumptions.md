# Assumptions

Every engineering decision that the paper (`main.tex`) does not specify is listed here with an ID. The IDs appear in code comments and configs (`# assumption A..`). Classification follows the project rule:

- **B**: reasonable engineering inference needed to make the system runnable;
- **C**: approximation, because the paper lacks the information for an exact implementation.

| ID | Class | What was missing | Decision | Why reasonable | How to change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | C | Topology and impedances of the synthetic Indian feeder (paper gives only 11 kV, 4 × 630 kVA, 248 LV buses, 1,340 households) | Radial LV network per transformer: `laterals_per_transformer` chains of equal segments, `segment_r_ohm = 0.006`, `segment_x_ohm = 0.002`; households spread evenly over buses | Reproduces the stated counts. With these values, base load alone stays within limits and uncontrolled charging produces overloads and undervoltage at full scale (verified: 163% peak, 727 violation bus-steps on one test day) | `feeder.*` in the config, or supply a feeder JSON (`feeder.source: json`) |
| A2 | C | Phase model, MV feeder, EV power factor | Each LV network is a balanced single-phase equivalent; the transformer secondary is the slack at 1.0 p.u. (the 11 kV drop is ignored); EVs run at unity power factor; household loads at pf 0.95 | The paper itself "approximates phase unbalance per phase"; per-phase equivalence keeps LinDistFlow and the AC check consistent | Replace `solace.grid` with a three-phase solver (e.g. OpenDSS or pandapower 3-ph) |
| A3 | B/C | How vehicles are sampled from Table II ranges; sessions per day | Battery size uniform in range; charger rating drawn uniformly from the listed values (2W {1.0, 3.3}, 3W {3.3, 7.4}, 4W {7.4, 30} kW); `sessions_per_day = 1`; aggregator = (transformer, vehicle index mod 2) | Simplest unbiased reading of Table II | `fleet.*` config |
| A4 | C | Functional form of the 2W/3W session generator; 4W fallback when ACN-Data is absent | Gaussian-mixture arrival times (2W mode at 18:00), log-normal dwell and energy with Table II means, plug-in SoC ~ U(0.1, 0.6), non-overlapping sessions per vehicle | Matches the stated 18:00 session peak and Table II means. **Note:** Table II's fleet (62% 2W vehicles, 15 kWh 4W sessions) cannot simultaneously reproduce the Bolt.Earth 78% 2W session share and 67% 4W energy share the paper cites; the implementation follows Table II | `fleet.<class>.arrival_modes`, `sessions_per_day`, or `data.acn_path` |
| A5 | B | Episode boundaries for 96-step days | Episode runs 08:00 → 08:00 next day so overnight 4W sessions fit; departures after the window are clipped to its end | Keeps evening peak and overnight valley in one episode | `data.episode_start_hour` |
| A6 | C | Base-load data, irradiance data, tariff values | Synthetic household profile with a 19:00–22:00 evening peak (1.4 kW household peak), synthetic clear-sky PV × daily factor U(0.4, 1) with 60 kW PV per transformer; ToD tariff normal 7.0 INR/kWh, peak 1.2× (paper), off-peak 0.8×, peak 18–22 h, off-peak 22–06 h | Paper gives only the shape and the 1.2× ratio | `data.*`; `data.irradiance_path` loads real hourly GHI |
| A7 | C | Distribution of user constraints during RL training (the paper trains on parsed intents from a corpus that is not released) | Constraints sampled per session: V2G opt-in p = 0.4 (V2G-capable classes), SoC floor U(0.3, 0.6), discharge cap 5–20% of capacity, 20% early deadlines, 25% cost / 10% urgent priority | Produces all four intent types the paper uses | `intent.sampling.*` |
| A8 | C | Degradation parameters (B, E_a, α, z, calendar model, temperature, battery cost) | Wang et al. (2011) functional form with A = 2.2 Ah reference-cell throughput, loss in %; B = 31630, E_a = 31700 J/mol, α = −370.3, z = 0.55 for both chemistries (NMC differs only in calendar ageing); 30 °C; 12,000 INR/kWh battery cost; end of life at 20% fade; batteries start each episode at 500 equivalent cycles | The paper names the models but not the values. Coefficients are from the cited model family and **must be checked against the sources** before quantitative use. The starting age matters because A^z is concave (a new battery makes the first cycles look very expensive) | `degradation.*` |
| A9 | C | How deadlines enter the reward ("soft terms"); reward scaling | Unmet energy at the deadline costs 30 INR/kWh in the per-step cost; reward = −0.001 × (energy + degradation + unmet cost) − β‖â − ã‖² with β = 0.05 (paper value) on normalized actions | Eq. (6) alone has no delivery incentive, which would make "never charge" optimal | `env.unmet_penalty_inr_per_kwh`, `env.reward_scale`, `env.beta_projection` |
| A10 | B | Behavior when Proposition 1's assumption fails (non-EV load alone violates a limit) | Rows violated at a = 0 are relaxed to A_j a ≤ 0 ("EVs may not make it worse"); the step is counted in `relaxed_steps` | Keeps the QP feasible and never lets EVs worsen an existing violation | `solace/safety/projection.py::build_transformer_problem` |
| A11 | C | Federated execution details and where the gradient "through (11)" enters PPO | Aggregators run sequentially in one process; each local iteration is a joint rollout in which every EV acts with its own aggregator's actor; each aggregator updates only on its own agents' transitions; the PPO ratio uses the sampled (pre-projection) action; the projection gradient enters through a term β‖Proj(μ) − μ‖² on the policy mean, differentiated via the KKT active set; per-aggregator Ĵ_F = 1 − Jain over its own sessions; d_D is normalized by the per-agent degradation of an uncontrolled (no-V2G) reference run. **Note:** Table III implies 300 × 5 × 32 × 96 ≈ 4.6 M environment steps, not the 20 M stated in the text; the code follows Table III | Faithful to Algorithm 1's structure while runnable on one machine | `solace/training/federated.py`, `solace/training/mappo.py` |
| A12 | C | Reward of the Thompson-sampling bandit baseline | Arm "charge" rewarded when own transformer loading < 0.9 and tariff ≤ normal; "idle" rewarded otherwise; urgency override near the deadline | The original paper is only cited | `solace/baselines/bandits.py` |
| A13 | C | Formulation of the MPC and solver-grounded baselines | Receding-horizon convex QP re-solved every step: energy cost + linear wear cost (marginal cost of eq. (7) at the configured battery age) + unmet-energy slack, under the same LinDistFlow, kVA-polygon and battery limits, small quadratic regularizer. MPC knows future arrivals and loads (perfect foresight); the solver-grounded agent sees only plugged-in EVs and uses yesterday's base load as forecast | Eq. (7) is not convex in the power trajectory; both baselines must share Layer 3's constraints | `solace/baselines/horizon_qp.py` |
| A14 | C | Headroom forecast used by the feasibility LP | The LP takes per-step headroom (kW) from the caller; `per_ev_headroom` splits transformer headroom over expected concurrent EVs; without headroom only charger and battery limits apply | Paper: "a forecast of local transformer headroom" without a model | `solace/intent/feasibility.py` |
| A15 | C | Grammar-constrained decoding; the LLM itself in tests | `HFParser` extracts the first JSON object and relies on the validator/retry loop instead of grammar-constrained decoding; tests and the default API use the deterministic `RuleBasedParser` stand-in (not the paper's method) | Model weights are gated and too large for CI | `intent.parser: hf` |
| A16 | C | Definition of "field F1" | Micro-averaged over the six fields; since all fields are always present it equals field accuracy | No definition in the paper | `solace/intent/metrics.py` |
| A17 | B | Exact metric definitions | Unmet energy and delivered ratio x_i measured at the session deadline; x_i clipped to [0, 1] for Jain; ISR = SoC ≥ min_soc − 0.01 and discharge cap respected; voltage violations = AC bus-steps outside [0.95, 1.05] p.u. by more than 1e-4; peak loading = max over transformers and steps of AC apparent power / rating | Paper gives verbal definitions | `solace/env/charging_env.py` |
| A18 | B | V2G price and power rating | Discharge credited at the import tariff; discharge rating equals charge rating (so p = a · P_rated is linear) | Keeps the projection a QP in the normalized action | `data.tariff.v2g_export_multiplier` |
| A19 | C | Penalty used by penalty-based baselines (MAPPO, IPPO, "w/o projection") | 50 INR per AC voltage-violation bus-step | Paper: "reward penalties for limit violations" without a value | `env.violation_penalty_inr` in `configs/baselines/*.yaml` |

## Added during the manuscript revision (2026-10-27)

| ID | Class | What was missing / wrong | Decision | How to change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A20 | B | With one dual update per round and `dual_lr = 1e-3`, λ cannot grow (audit C3, reviewer R1-02) | `training.dual_normalize` divides the violation by the budget; the study uses `dual_lr = 0.05` and `lambda_max = 100`. The paper's original value remains the default in `default.yaml` for traceability, but is documented as ineffective | `training.dual_*` |
| A21 | C | Projection-gradient term solves one QP per minibatch sample, which is too slow on a CPU | `training.projection_grad_batch = 16` estimates the term on a random 16-sample subset of each minibatch (an unbiased but noisier estimate) | `training.projection_grad_batch: null` uses all samples |
| A22 | C | MPC bound had no fairness term (reviewer R1-05) | `mpc_fair` adds a max-min delivered-fraction variable (convex surrogate of Jain) weighted by `unmet_penalty_inr_per_kwh` × total request | `FairMPCPolicy` |
| A23 | B | Solver-grounded baseline leaked future load on its first day (audit C4) | Fallback forecast = mean training-split base-load profile | `SolverGroundedPolicy(cfg, scen)` |
| A24 | C | Full-scale experiments infeasible on CPU | Reduced-scale study `configs/study.yaml`: one 250 kVA transformer, 24 LV buses, 120 households, 80 EVs (50/18/12), 60 days (40/10/10), 150 rounds × 4 environments, 3 seeds | `configs/study*.yaml` |
| A25 | B | Evaluation counted deterministic methods once per seed (audit C2) | Unit of analysis is the test day; seed-dependent methods are averaged per day; Wilcoxon signed-rank test and Cohen's d_z vs SOLACE | `solace/evaluation/runner.py` |
